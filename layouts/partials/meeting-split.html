{{/* Splits meetings into upcoming/past and returns them as a dict. A meeting counts as upcoming until its endDate has passed (falling back to its start time when endDate is missing), so it stays on the homepage for the whole evening it's happening rather than disappearing at 6:30 PM. Compared as Unix seconds so the check doesn't depend on how the frontmatter endDate was parsed. Returns: dict "upcoming" (soonest first) "past" (newest first). Past is capped at the 4 most recent so the Meetings page doesn't grow without bound at two meetings a month. Upcoming is uncapped — we never have many. */ -}} {{- $pastLimit := 4 -}} {{- $nowUnix := now.Unix -}} {{- $upcoming := slice -}} {{- $past := slice -}} {{- range where site.RegularPages "Section" "meetings" -}} {{- $end := .Date -}} {{- with .Params.endDate }}{{ $end = time . }}{{ end -}} {{- if ge $end.Unix $nowUnix -}} {{- $upcoming = $upcoming | append . -}} {{- else -}} {{- $past = $past | append . -}} {{- end -}} {{- end -}} {{- return dict "upcoming" (sort $upcoming "Date" "asc") "past" (first $pastLimit (sort $past "Date" "desc")) -}}
