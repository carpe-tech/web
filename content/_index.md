## What we're about

CARPE is a local community of makers, hobbyists, and tech enthusiasts who get together
in person **twice a month** to explore projects built around Arduino, Raspberry&nbsp;Pi,
and other microcontrollers — along with retro computing, electronics, and creative tech.

Whether you just unboxed your first kit or you've been soldering since the 1970s, you'll
find a welcoming group that loves to learn, share, and tinker. There's no membership and
no dues — you're part of the community the moment you show up.
