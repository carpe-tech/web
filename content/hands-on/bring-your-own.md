Bring your breadboard, microcontrollers and sensors — a Pico W, an ESP32, an Arduino, whatever
you're working with. Finished project or a bag of parts, it all fits. And bring your laptop, to
work on code and flash your devices.

## An optional backend: the Mobile Factory

For multi-device projects, the **Deevnet Mobile Factory** — a portable IoT platform one of our
members brings to meetings — gives your devices a network and ready-made back-end services:

- **MQTT messaging**, with an account per device
- **Logs**, and **dashboards** to chart them
- All declared from your own Terraform, with **no cloud account** and nothing leaving the room

It's optional: plenty of projects need nothing but a USB cable.

- [Before You Start](https://deevnet.github.io/deevnet-docs/docs/runbook/tenant/getting-started/before-you-start/) —
