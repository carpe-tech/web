Bring your breadboard, microcontrollers and sensors — a Pico W, an ESP32, an Arduino, whatever
you're working with. Finished project or a bag of parts, it all fits. And bring your laptop, to
work on code and flash your devices ([get your laptop ready](/hands-on/laptop-setup/)).

Plenty of projects need nothing but a USB cable. For multi-device projects that need a network
and a backend, see [On-Site IoT as a Service](/hands-on/on-site-iot/).
