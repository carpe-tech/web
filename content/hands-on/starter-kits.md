New to electronics, or just curious about tinkering? A couple of **starter kits** are on hand
at meetings so you can try things out:

- Blink your first LED
- Put something on a display
- Play with a few simple sensors

No pressure, no experience required — just an interest in learning and building. Bring a
laptop and we'll help you get set up, or [get your laptop ready](/hands-on/laptop-setup/)
before you come.

## The kit

Our starter kits are a Raspberry Pi Pico basic starter kit
([on Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/dp/B0C3771CK8)). It supports MicroPython, C/C++ and Piper
Make, and comes with tutorials and sample code.

{{< alert "circle-info" >}}
**No Wi-Fi.** The kit's Pico is not a Pico W, so it can't connect to a network. If your project
needs Wi-Fi, there may be a spare Pico W or another Wi-Fi-enabled microcontroller available —
just ask.
{{< /alert >}}
