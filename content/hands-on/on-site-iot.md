Some projects outgrow a USB cable: several devices that need to talk to each other, and
somewhere to send their data. For those, one of our members brings the **Deevnet Mobile
Factory** to meetings, a portable IoT platform that is its own mini-cloud. It gives you, on site:

- **A predictable network** for your devices, with a Wi-Fi key of your own
- **MQTT messaging**, with an account per device
- **Logs**, and **dashboards** to chart them
- All declared from your own Terraform, with **no cloud account** and nothing leaving the room

It works with [your own devices](/hands-on/bring-your-own/) and with a borrowed
[project kit](/hands-on/project-kits/), which joins as a device in your own space and goes back
at the end of the night with nothing of yours left on it.

It's optional. You only need it when your project needs a backend.
