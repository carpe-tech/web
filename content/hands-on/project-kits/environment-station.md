## What it does

Reads temperature, humidity, pressure and light, and publishes them so they can be charted on a dashboard.

## What it teaches

Sense and publish: the simplest useful IoT pattern, and the quickest route to a first dashboard.

## Parts (planned)

- Temperature, humidity and pressure sensor (BME280)
- Light sensor
