## What it does

An I2S microphone measures sound levels in the room, and the levels are charted over time.

## What it teaches

Streaming audio data, and turning a stream into something worth charting. It pairs well with the Motion Show's I2S amplifier.

## Parts (planned)

- I2S microphone (INMP441)
