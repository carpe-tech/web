---
title: "Community Guidelines"
description: "A few simple ground rules that keep CARPE welcoming and fun."
---

CARPE works because people are friendly, curious, and generous with their time. A few
ground rules keep it that way:

## Be welcoming

Everyone started somewhere. Newcomers and beginners are the lifeblood of this group —
treat them like it.

## No gatekeeping

There are no dumb questions. Every "obvious" thing was once a mystery to someone now
holding a soldering iron. Share what you know without making anyone feel small for not
knowing it yet.

## Respect people, space, and gear

We're often guests in a borrowed room. Clean up after yourself, ask before touching
someone's project, and treat venues and equipment with care so we're invited back.

## Keep it safe and legal

We play with mains power, lithium batteries, RF, lasers, and other things that can bite.
Know what you're doing — or ask someone who does — before you do it. Don't bring anything
to a meeting you wouldn't want your most safety-conscious friend to see.

## When in doubt

Be the kind of person you'd want sitting next to you at a workbench. That covers almost
everything.
